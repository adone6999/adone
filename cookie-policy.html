1. What are cookies & similar technologies

Cookies are small text files a website can place on your device to remember information between pages or visits. Similar technologies include browser local storage and session storage, which let a site store small amounts of data in your browser. We refer to all of these together as "cookies and similar technologies" in this policy.

2. How AdOne uses them

This website uses only a small number of essential cookies and local-storage items — those strictly necessary for the site to function and to respect your choices. Specifically:

  • A consent record stored in your browser's localStorage under the key adone_cookie_consent, so we remember your cookie choice and don't ask again on every visit.
  • Short-lived storage used only on the secure account-deletion web flow — to hold your session while you complete that flow. It is not used for tracking, and it is cleared when the flow ends.

We do not use cookies for analytics, profiling or advertising.

3. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies

AdOne does not use third-party advertising cookies and does not use cross-site tracking cookies. We do not build advertising profiles of you, and we do not share your browsing for third-party advertising. The only cookies and storage we use are the essential ones described above.

4. The mobile apps

The AdOne mobile apps do not use browser cookies. Instead, they use secure on-device storage to keep your session tokens and preferences on your own device. This data stays on your device under the app's protected storage and is used to keep you signed in and to remember your settings — not to track you across other apps or sites.

6. Managing cookies

You can view, manage and delete cookies and local-storage data through your browser settings — most browsers let you clear stored data and block or limit cookies for a site. Please note that because the items we use are essential, declining or clearing them may limit features: for example, we may have to ask for your cookie choice again, or the secure account-deletion web flow may not work correctly. You can still delete your account in-app (see GDPR Compliance).
