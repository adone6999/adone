Compliance

Last updated: 24 June 2026

This page sets out the compliance posture of ("AdOne", "we", "us") and links the policies that govern the AdOne app and this website.

In short. AdOne operates as an intermediary under Indian law. We connect advertisers with advertising-space owners and help facilitate a deal between them; we are not a party to the advertising contract and we process no online payments. We follow intermediary due-diligence obligations, protect personal data, run a grievance-redressal mechanism, and moderate content and takedown requests.

1. Overview

AdOne is a platform for advertising space in India. We list space owners' advertising space, qualify advertisers' inquiries, and help facilitate a deal between the parties. Because we operate in India and handle personal data and user-generated content, we maintain the compliance practices described here. This page is informational and should be read together with the policies it links to.

2. Regulatory framework

Our compliance is shaped principally by the following Indian laws and rules:

FrameworkWhat it covers for AdOne
Information Technology Act, 2000 & IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021Our status and safe-harbour as an intermediary, our due-diligence duties, publication of terms and policies, and a Grievance Officer with defined response timelines.
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)Lawful processing of personal data, notice and consent where required, data-principal rights, and our obligations as a data fiduciary.
Consumer Protection Act, 2019Fair dealing and transparency in how the marketplace operates, and clear disclosure of AdOne's intermediary role.
GST / tax lawApplies to the deals settled between the transacting parties; those parties handle their own invoicing and tax.

3. Intermediary status & due diligence

AdOne is an intermediary: we host and transmit listings and inquiries provided by users, and we help facilitate a deal between the parties. We are not a party to the advertising contract between an advertiser and a space owner, and the Platform processes no online payments — see our Terms of Service › AdOne's role as intermediary.

As part of intermediary due diligence we:

  • Publish and keep current our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and acceptable-use rules, and require users to agree to them.
  • Inform users of content that is not permitted, and act on valid complaints and takedown requests.
  • Appoint a Grievance Officer and publish their contact details (see section 4).
  • Retain records and respond to lawful requests from authorised government agencies (see section 7).

5. Data protection & privacy

We process personal data lawfully and collect only what we need to run the service. Phone numbers are the identity for app users (OTP sign-in, no passwords). For full detail, and your rights including account deletion, see our Privacy Policy and GDPR Compliance pages.

6. Content moderation & takedown

We act on reports of policy-violating, unlawful or infringing content. We may remove content, suspend accounts and notify the relevant party. Use the right channel for your report:

  • Abuse, unsafe or policy-violating content — see our Abuse Policy (report to ).
  • Copyright or other IP infringement — see our IPR Complaints process ().

7. Law-enforcement & legal requests

We respond to lawful requests from courts and authorised government and law-enforcement agencies. We require that requests are valid, specific and made under proper legal authority, and we disclose only the data the request reasonably calls for. Where permitted by law, we may notify the affected user. Requests should be sent to .

8. Security

We protect the Platform with measures including OTP sign-in (no passwords), short-lived sessions, encryption in transit and access controls on our systems. For details and how to report a vulnerability, see our Security page or contact .

9. Records, audit & retention

We keep records only as long as needed for the purposes for which they were collected or as required by law. When a user deletes their account, we erase personal details and free the phone number, while retaining de-identified records of completed deals and inquiries for legitimate legal, tax, accounting and audit purposes. See Privacy Policy › Retention for more.
